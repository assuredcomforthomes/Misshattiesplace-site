The Experience

Minutes from downtown Beaufort.
A fire pit to gather the family after graduation day.

There's no better feeling than walking back in after graduation day — dress blues still hanging by the door, pecan smoke drifting up from the fire pit, crickets starting their evening hum past the fence — and finally having the whole family under one roof.

Most families who come through this door are in Beaufort for one reason: to watch someone they love graduate from Parris Island. That day deserves more than a hotel room off the highway. Park in the driveway beneath the pecan tree, and in three minutes you're walking brick sidewalks still warm from the afternoon sun, past live oaks dripping in Spanish moss, toward galleries and the low murmur of dinner crowds on Bay Street. Walk back and the house is single-level and step-free throughout, so grandparents, toddlers, and everyone in between move through it just as easily.

Once the uniforms come off and the photos are taken, the evening belongs to the backyard — the low crackle of the gas fire pit, bare feet on cool grass, the smell of woodsmoke drifting while the stars come out over the Lowcountry. Inside, a 55-inch TV, fast 231 Mbps wifi, and a fully equipped kitchen keep nine guests fed and entertained, with two full bathrooms and four bedrooms so nobody's waiting in line.

What's Around

the Lowcountry unfolds.

Hattie's Place sits inside the Beaufort Historic District: close enough to walk downtown for dinner, close enough to drive to Parris Island for a graduation morning.

10-Minute Drive

Hunting Island State Park

Palmettos lean into salt wind and a barrier-island beach stretches for miles beyond them, the kind of wild coastline that makes a South Carolina vacation feel like one.

3-Minute Walk

Beaufort Historic District

Antebellum porches and live oaks draped in moss line the streets just past the front door, a few unhurried blocks to galleries, dinner, and the river.

5-Minute Walk

Henry C. Chambers Waterfront Park

Swing benches face the marsh as shrimp boats idle past, five minutes on foot to an evening that slows down on its own.

8-Minute Drive

Cypress Wetlands Rookery

Hundreds of egrets and herons nest right over a quiet boardwalk in downtown Port Royal, a Lowcountry secret most visitors never hear about.

20-Minute Drive

Parris Island Marine Corps Recruit Depot

Families gather here to watch recruits graduate and earn the title of Marine, a milestone that brings many guests to Beaufort in the first place.

5-Minute Drive

Beaufort National Cemetery

Rows of white headstones rest beneath old oaks on one of the nation's oldest military cemeteries, a solemn, peaceful stop just outside downtown.

Good to know

Questions guests ask before booking

What time is check-in and checkout at Hattie's Place?

Check-in is after 4:00 PM with self check-in through a smart lock, so you can arrive on your own schedule. Checkout is by 10:00 AM. Quiet hours run from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM.

Are pets allowed at Hattie's Place?

No, Hattie's Place does not allow pets or smoking anywhere on the property, indoors or out. The home sleeps up to 9 guests across four bedrooms and two bathrooms.

How far is Hattie's Place from Parris Island?

It's about a 20-minute drive to Parris Island MCRD, which makes Hattie's Place a popular base for Marine Corps graduation weekends. Downtown Beaufort itself is a 3-minute walk from the front door.

Is there parking at the property?

Yes. Hattie's Place has free parking on site for up to 6 vehicles, plus free street parking for up to 3 additional cars, which is useful for larger family groups traveling together.

Can I book directly instead of through Airbnb?

Yes. This page lets you check live availability and book directly with Lee, who manages the home and your reservation personally from start to finish.
